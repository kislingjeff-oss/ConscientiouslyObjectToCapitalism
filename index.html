Great Plains Action Society

One

The System

“A system that was built by stolen bodies on stolen land for the benefit of a few is a system that is not repairable.” — Ronnie James, Des Moines Mutual Aid

Land enclosed and taken from Indigenous nations; chattel slavery supplying stolen labor. Together, an interlocking system.

The engine of an astounding transfer of wealth to the ultra rich, and the engine destroying Mother Earth.

Two

The Reckoning

“Quakers are too comfortable living in a capitalist system that is inherently unjust.”

Most white Quakers believe colonialism is a thing of the past, the greatest obstacle to change. Churches, Quakers included, ran Indian boarding schools.

Incremental efforts fail because they leave the systemic injustices in place.

Three

The Way Out

“Mutual Aid can be the Quaker way of being in the world today.”

LANDBACK, Mutual Aid, and abolition of police and prisons.

Weekly free food distribution in Des Moines, carrying on the Black Panthers’ Free Breakfast for School Children. Tool libraries that share what no one person needs to own.

The query

Do we continue to accept the current dominant systems of colonial capitalist violence?
